# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical operators running Ollama locally or on a private machine. They use the dashboard during model loading, inference, troubleshooting, and capacity checks, and need to understand system health without decoding an undifferentiated wall of metrics.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable operational view of Ollama: loaded models, live compute and host health, inference activity, local inventory, jobs, controls, and configuration. Success means an operator can identify current state and anything requiring attention in seconds, then inspect full detail without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The interface should feel trustworthy and technically fluent without looking like a novelty terminal or a generic analytics template.

## Anti-references

Avoid equal-weight card walls, decorative dashboard chrome, excessive status color, tiny low-contrast text, and terminal styling that makes every label and value compete equally. Do not hide or remove data to manufacture simplicity.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with health: loaded models, resource pressure, service state, and active work must be readable at a glance.
2. Separate signal from evidence: show immediate state first, then preserve complete tables and diagnostic detail nearby.
3. Use density deliberately: compact rows are appropriate for inventories and logs, but section hierarchy must remain unmistakable.
4. Make status explicit: pair semantic color with text, stable placement, and clear labels.
5. Keep operations predictable: familiar controls, visible focus states, and consistent feedback should support fast, safe use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction conventions. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, non-color status cues, readable default type sizes, responsive reflow, and horizontal containment for genuinely wide data tables.
